Sound effects (camera whoosh, new-leader chime, subscribe click) are generated in the browser and mixed into the video automatically. Music isn't saved with the project because of its size, so upload it again when you reopen a project.

Export doesn't record the screen: every frame is rendered precisely and encoded straight to MP4 (H.264 when available), so there's no stutter even on slow machines, and you can export in 4K. Chrome or Edge work best.
